**Favourite food**
I dont have a favourite food. I enjoy food above all, I think it is the best thing is life. However, I dont have one single food that rules it all. I am however drawn to curries, and Asian dishes in general, the flavour profile of the east is just amazing. I love the umami flavours of mushroom, soy sauce, miso, gochujang and the mixing of something savoury with sweet and spicey (just a bit though). I cook from scratch, I always have, and I  aim to cook something different every day (thats just the aim though). I want to try everything that exists - within limits though as I dont consider animals food. 
I really enjoy experimenting with crazy recipes, such as making a cake from millet, or chickpeas.
